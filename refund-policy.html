Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

We want you to be happy with your seva essentials. If something isn't right, here's how we can help.

1. Returns window

You may request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and in the same condition you received it.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, opened beauty, bathing, and natural care products cannot be returned unless defective or incorrect.

2. How to request a return

Contact us at littlegopal7@gmail.com or on WhatsApp with your order number and reason for return. We'll confirm the pickup or return shipping details with you.

3. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we'll notify you of the approval status. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer or UPI.

4. Damaged or incorrect items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item — we'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you.

5. Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once shipped, please follow the returns process above instead.

6. Shipping costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (damaged, defective, or incorrect item).

7. Contact us

For any questions about returns or refunds, email littlegopal7@gmail.com or reach us on WhatsApp.
