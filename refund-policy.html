Replacement Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Little Gopal does not offer returns or refunds. We do offer a replacement if an item arrives defective or with a genuine quality issue.

No returns or refunds

We are unable to accept returns or process refunds for any reason, including change of mind or incorrect size selection. Please review product details and the size guide carefully before ordering.

1. When a replacement is offered

A replacement is offered only when the item you receive is defective or has a genuine quality issue.

2. Replacement window

You must request a replacement within 10–12 days of the delivery date. Requests made after this window cannot be accepted.

3. How to request a replacement

Email us at littlegopal7@gmail.com with your order number, a description of the defect/quality issue, and clear photos of the item. We'll review your request and confirm next steps.

4. Replacement time

Once your replacement request is approved, please allow 7–10 business days to receive your replacement item.

5. Contact us

For any questions about this policy, email littlegopal7@gmail.com.
