Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Little Gopal ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you shop with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number, when you create an account or check out.
  • Order details — delivery address, items purchased, order value, and payment status (we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details — those are handled directly by our payment partner).
  • Usage data — pages visited and products viewed, to help us improve the store.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations and updates
  • To respond to customer support requests
  • To improve our products and website experience

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment gateway partner. We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or banking passwords — these are handled securely by the payment provider under their own security standards.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart, account details, and order history on this device, so you don't have to re-enter information each visit. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at littlegopal7@gmail.com.
